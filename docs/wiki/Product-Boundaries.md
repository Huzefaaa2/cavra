# Product Boundaries

This boundary keeps CAVRA Community useful while protecting private operations.

## Public

- CAVRA Community source.
- Public contracts and schemas.
- Runtime decisions, policy evaluation, approvals, evidence, AISPM, report center, dashboards, and CI/CD enforcement.
- Connector SDK and reference connectors.
- Documentation, textbook, examples, and public-safe validators.

## Private Or Commercial

- CAVRA Managed service implementation and operations.
- Customer tenant records.
- Commercial policy packs and compliance packs.
- Certified connector packages.
- Support records, billing records, procurement records, and customer-success operating notes.
- Private customer runbooks, customer evidence, and customer templates.
- License signing material, package tokens, production connector credentials, and secrets.
