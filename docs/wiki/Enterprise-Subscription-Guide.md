# CAVRA Enterprise Subscription Guide

CAVRA Enterprise Subscription is a commercial relationship, not a separate
source edition.

Enterprise Subscription can include:

- Commercial support and SLA.
- Certified connectors.
- Commercial policy packs and compliance packs.
- Implementation help and custom integrations.
- Procurement and security review support.
- Private customer operations and deployment assistance.

Enterprise Subscription can support self-hosted CAVRA Community, CAVRA Managed,
or a hybrid deployment model.
