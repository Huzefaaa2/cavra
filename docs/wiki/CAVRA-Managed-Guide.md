# CAVRA Managed Guide

CAVRA Managed is hosted CAVRA operated as a managed service. It is the right path
when a team wants CAVRA operated for them instead of running every backing
service themselves.

## Managed Responsibilities

CAVRA Managed can provide:

- Hosted tenant onboarding and tenant operations.
- Managed policy registry.
- Managed dashboards.
- Managed report delivery.
- Managed audit storage.
- Updates, monitoring, uptime operations, support handoff, and customer-success operating review.
- Billing and service operations.

Managed-service implementation, customer records, credentials, support data,
and operational runbooks remain outside the public repository.
