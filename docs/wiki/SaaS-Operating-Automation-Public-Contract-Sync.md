## Public Boundary

Public documentation may describe:

- private PR numbers and public-safe capability names;
- high-level readiness, closure, handoff, summary, and governance concepts;
- user stories and enterprise value;
- public contract surfaces and expected private-service boundaries;
- evidence categories without payload contents.

Public documentation must not include:

- Enterprise source code;
- SaaS backend source or implementation design;
- automation worker, scheduler, or connector implementation;
- customer records, account notes, customer health scores, or support tickets;
- billing records, invoices, license keys, or signing material;
- production dashboard URLs, provider endpoints, webhooks, or account IDs;
- compliance workpapers, executive notes, incident contents, or rollback runbooks;
- private package paths, paid policy packs, or private policy registry logic.
